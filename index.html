Chapter I 01 — About

A salon that feels like your salon.

Opium is a small, independent unisex salon tucked into the corner of St Peters Road, where the regulars know each other and the kettle is rarely off.

We're a hair salon first — cuts, colour, blow-dries, the works — but over the years we've grown to look after a bit of everything: beauty, nails, foot care, and a few quietly brilliant holistic therapies for when life gets noisy.

What we won't do is rush you. Walk through the door and you'll get a proper consultation, an honest opinion, and a cup of something hot while we work.

In the chair

Julia & the team

Long-standing stylists who've built their reputation the slow way — one good cut, one returning client at a time. Bring a picture, bring nothing at all, or bring a problem you've been growing out for six months. We've seen worse, and we'll sort it.
